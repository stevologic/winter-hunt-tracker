PUBLIC DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

The work ahead.

We’re continuing work on Winter Hunt’s features, online systems and testing. Here is what has been completed and what still needs attention.

Work in progress. Completed private-build fixes and automated checks do not establish human-play, hardware or release readiness.

Completed / scoped work

Private-build fixes and automated checks

Build49 private-build fixes and automated checks have been completed. The validation areas below remain pending.

In development

Features still being built

  • Live voice: in development. Service setup, connection recovery and live acceptance remain to be completed.
  • Online leaderboards: in development. Opt-in online submission and ranking still need service integration and validation.
  • Third-level zombie elk: work on zombie elk for the fire level is in development.
  • Particle-rich visual effects: dedicated work across biomes, environments, the interface, weapons and impacts.
  • Playing with friends: improve the hosting and joining experience.
Pending validation

Real-world play and hardware

  • Human playtesting of campaign progression, encounters and the full friends experience.
  • Input, accessibility, controller and Steam Deck validation on actual devices.
  • Networking between separate PCs.
  • Audio and performance validation.
  • Targeted acceptance checks for reward and save recovery.
  • Validation of which player classes are available in the production build.
  • Shipping package, clean installation, update, restart and rollback checks.
Pending

Platform onboarding

Platform onboarding remains pending. Store-readiness work includes supported-feature descriptions, privacy and service disclosures, and measured hardware requirements.
