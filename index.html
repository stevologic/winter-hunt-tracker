TWO BROTHERS / ONE CHAOTIC HUNT

Help us build the next hunt.

Winter Hunt started with a simple idea: make a game we can play with our friends. We’re building toward co-op hunts that get stranger, louder and more chaotic as the night goes on.

Our initial targets are PC and Steam Deck; Steam Deck support still needs testing and is not verified. An iOS version is a later aspiration. We’re aiming for the Steam Winter Sale if development progress, readiness and approvals allow, with no guaranteed release date.

    WINTER STEAM SALE

    December 17, 2026
    through January 4, 2027.

    Official event window. Exact start hour has not been verified. Participation depends on quality, approvals and account eligibility.

    Confirm the account clocks

    Valve currently requires 21 days after the app fee for the first few titles, plus at least two weeks of public Coming Soon. The owner's actual dates and approvals still need private confirmation.

    Review before release

    Store review comes first. Typical store and build reviews take 3–5 business days; allow at least 7 business days for each. A source-only candidate is not a playable review build.

    Eligibility is evidence

    A game must have been released at least 30 days before the Winter Sale starts to discount. Seasonal sales do not waive the price-increase cooldown. Participation needs confirmed dashboard eligibility and a saved discount. A public playtest is not a Steam release.

    Planning targets · not promises

    November 16 is a buffered planning target, not an announced release date. The plan depends on a validated build, real player feedback and completed Steam approvals. If those gates remain open, the release target moves.

    AI artwork shipped to players needs pre-generated AI disclosure in the Content Survey. Website concept art is not automatically in-game content.
