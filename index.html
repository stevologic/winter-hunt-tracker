    WINTER STEAM SALE

    through January 4, 2027.

    Official event window. Exact start hour has not been verified. Participation depends on quality, approvals and account eligibility.

    Two clocks, one gate

    A first title needs 21 days after app-fee payment and at least two weeks of public Coming Soon. The clocks can overlap. Identity submission does not start the fee clock.

    Review before release

    Store review comes first. Typical store and build reviews take 3–5 business days; allow at least 7 business days for each. A source-only candidate is not a playable review build.

    Eligibility is evidence

    Seasonal discounts require the 30-day release and price-increase cooldowns. Launch discounts are 10–40% for 7–14 days and must be configured before release. Conflicting launch-cooldown wording requires dashboard verification.

    Planning targets · not promises

    November 17 is the calculated 30-day boundary; November 16 adds buffer. If quality slips, a December release may be available during the sale with an eligible launch discount, without opening seasonal-discount eligibility. Never rush an unfinished build.

    AI artwork shipped to players needs pre-generated AI disclosure in the Content Survey. Website concept art is not automatically in-game content.
