TWO BROTHERS / ONE CHAOTIC HUNT

Help us build the next hunt.

Winter Hunt started with a simple idea: make a game we can play with our friends. We’re building toward co-op hunts that get stranger, louder and more chaotic as the night goes on.

Our initial targets are PC and Steam Deck; Steam Deck support still needs testing and is not verified. An iOS version is a later aspiration. We’re aiming for the Steam Winter Sale if development progress, readiness and approvals allow, with no guaranteed release date.

WORK-IN-PROGRESS TESTING

Request playtest access.

Playtest builds are shared through a restricted Google Drive folder. Sign in with your Google account, open the folder and request access. Access requires owner approval and is granted individually.

Request playtest access on Google Drive

The current verified private Windows playtest candidate is Build81. It adds an American flag at camp, a larger Normal-mode map, clearer Zombie area cues, and visual updates. Local automated checks have passed; human play, hardware, and separate-PC online testing remain open.

These builds are a work in progress and are still being tested. Donations are voluntary and independent of playtest access; donating does not guarantee approval or a copy of the finished game.

WORK IN PROGRESS

A game we’re still building.

We’re working on clearer encounters, camp activities and cooperative hunts. Some early checks have passed, but everyday play, separate-PC multiplayer, controller support, Steam Deck compatibility and sustained performance still need testing.

PC and Steam Deck are our initial targets. Steam Deck support is not verified. An iOS version is a later aspiration.

We’re aiming for the Steam Winter Sale if progress, readiness and approvals allow. There is no guaranteed release date.
