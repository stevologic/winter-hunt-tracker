# Genuine game captures

`actual-camp.webp` and `actual-marshmallow.webp` are optimized from existing Winter Hunt player captures at historical checkpoint `8e300c5`. The source captures were inspected locally; no fresh game/browser session was launched and no licensed raw assets were extracted. They show the actual flat-shaded camp, conifers, bow and activity signage.

These screenshots are displayed under the user's instruction to use genuine game graphics. They are labeled historical and are not evidence of current-package feature acceptance. They were not supplied to an AI image model.
